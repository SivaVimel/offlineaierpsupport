Getting started

1

Open the app and wait a few seconds while the on-device AI loads.

2

Enter your company name, choose your country and currency. Tax rates are filled in for many countries.

3

Add your logo and company details in More → Settings. They appear on every document.

4

Add employees, clients and suppliers — one by one, by bulk CSV import, or by asking the AI.

Just looking? Choose “Explore with a sample company” to try every module with a year of realistic data. Each time you reopen the app you'll be offered the choice again, and starting your own company clears the sample completely.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Every feature, including the AI assistant, works fully offline. Your data never leaves your device unless you share a document or export a backup.

My country or currency isn't the default. Can I change it?

Yes. Every country and every currency is available. Search for yours in setup, or later in More → Settings → Currency and VAT & taxes. Amounts use the correct number of decimals for each currency.

How do I set up VAT, GST or my own tax?

Go to More → Settings → VAT & taxes. Pick your country to load its standard rates, or tap “Add custom tax” to create any rate with your own name and percentage. Mark one as the default for new documents. Built-in rates are a starting point — please confirm them with your tax adviser.

How do I put my logo and details on invoices and quotations?

In More → Settings, open Company to enter your name, address, tax number, bank details and signatory, and Logo to add your logo. They appear on quotations, LPOs, invoices, payslips and every report.

What can I ask the AI assistant?

Tap the sparkle icon at the top right of any screen. Try:

  • “Who is on leave today?” or “What's our cash position?”
  • “Overdue invoices” or “Profit this month”
  • “Add leave for Ravi from tomorrow for 3 days”
  • “Increase E0005 basic pay to 4500”

Before adding or changing anything, it shows exactly what it will save and waits for you to confirm. Every figure comes from your own records.

Can the AI delete records?

No. Deleting is always done by you in the relevant screen. The assistant offers a shortcut to open that screen instead.

How do I stop the AI while it's writing?

Tap Stop on the card or in the chat. Tap Start to write it again. Writing also stops automatically when you move to another screen.

How does payroll work?

Open People → Payroll → New pay run and choose the month. Payslips are calculated from each employee's pay setup, approved leave, absences, overtime, one-off entries and advance recovery. Review the draft, approve it to post it to the accounts, then mark it paid once salaries are transferred. The bank list and payslips are available as PDFs.

Why can't I edit an older invoice or entry?

Its accounting month has been closed to protect your figures. Reopen the month in Assets → Accounting → Periods, make the change, then close it again.

How do the barcodes work?

Each employee, asset and vehicle has a printable barcode label. Tap the scanner icon at the top of any screen and point the camera at a label to open that record instantly. You can also type the code.

Can I import my existing data?

Yes. In More → Settings → Bulk import you can download a CSV template for employees, assets, clients, suppliers or vehicles, fill it in a spreadsheet and import it. A preview shows which rows will be imported and explains any that are skipped.

How do I move to a new iPhone or iPad?

On the old device go to More → Settings → Backup & restore and export a backup file. Send it to the new device (for example with AirDrop or Files), then choose Restore there.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad the layout uses the full screen: wider tables, side-by-side statements and charts, and every orientation.

The app takes a few seconds to open. Is that normal?

Yes. The AI model is loaded into memory each time the app opens, shown by the “AI is loading” screen. It's quickest on recent devices. All your data is ready as soon as the app opens.

How do I remove all my data?

Go to More → Settings → Data → Erase all data and type ERASE to confirm. Your company settings are kept; every record is removed. Deleting the app removes everything.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. About 1 GB of free storage is recommended.
